Patterns & the Card

16 patterns, A and B sides; each holds a bassline and a drum part, and the two sides can play different patterns
Edits save to /X0X by themselves, unless write-protected
Import a MIDI file: name it after a pattern (3B.mid), put it in /X0X/IMPORT on the card and power on. Channel 10 becomes the drums, everything else the bassline

Bass & Drums

Both are always playing/stopped: use LOOP (in Step Mode) or CHOMPI double-tap to switch between bass/drums edit
CHOMPI + LOOP (Step Mode): mutes the current side you're on
VOLUME knob: Pg. 3: Mix · CHOMPI: FX Balance
Pg. 4: Compressor · CHOMPI: Side-Chain: kick ducks the rest

Lights

Steps red; accent bright, tie dim · patterns A / B, magenta / orange protected
CHOMPI and LOOP purple on the bass, amber on the drums; LOOP blinks if muted
PLAY green while running · LOOP red while recording

LFO Targets · CHOMPI + Knob 1, Pg 4

1 off · 2 level · 3 pan 4 tuning (+/- octave) · 5 filter: low-pass, swept down from open 6 feedback FM · 7 decay
LFO Shapes: off, triangle, ramp up, ramp down, sample & hold. The LFO restarts when you press PLAY, so it always lines up with the start of the pattern

Sound

In every pattern, each drum part has its own length (polymeters)
Accent: up to 3× as loud · a closed hat chokes the open one
A plate reverb; the delay is shared with the bass, so the echoes line up
MIDI channel 10, GM notes: BD 36 · SD 38 · LT 45 · HT 50 · CY 49 · OH 46 · CH 42

Lights

Voice keys in their colours; the selected voice is bright; each voice LED flashes as it plays
Muted: voice blinks slowly · whole kit unmuted: all flash white
Accent blue · roll green · erase red, with the voices it's erasing
CHOMPI amber on this side; it flashes on the beat
