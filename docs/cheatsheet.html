Step Mode toggle up build the pattern one step at a time

Step key
select a step; again: on / off. Middle C is step 8 or 9
CHOMPI + any key
set the selected step's note (the keybed is a keyboard)
DOWN … TIE
a page: step keys toggle octave down / up, accent, slide, tie
TRANSPOSE
keys set the transpose. Leave: tap a key twice, or hold 2 s
PATTERN
the bass's pattern page: A, or B with CHOMPI held. Running, a pick waits for the bar; again: now
PATTERN held + key
on the page: both bass and drums there, together at the bar
PATTERN held 2 s
write protect, while stopped: play freely, nothing saves
Pattern key held 2 s
export every pattern as MIDI to /X0X/MIDI
COPY + step key
copy the pattern there · + PATTERN: to its other side, A ⇄ B
CLEAR
tap: the selected step · hold 1 s: the pattern
LOOP
switch to the drums · CHOMPI + LOOP: mute / unmute the bass

Live Mode toggle down play, record, arpeggiate

Keys

LOOP
record on / off · hold 2 s: clear the pattern
Recording, running
notes go in, held notes tie. CHOMPI + accent / slide / tie: on the step playing
Recording, stopped
note entry: each note fills the next step. CHOMPI + the blinking step: a rest
QUANTIZE
on / off · CHOMPI + white key 1–3: grid 1/16, 1/8, 1/4 · hold 2 s: for good
CHOMPI + PLAY

LFO Targets · Knob 1, Pg 4

1 off · 2 level: dips it, as far as silent · 3 pan: swings it either side
4 tuning: an octave either way · 5 filter: its own low-pass, swept down from open
6 feedback FM: adds grit · 7 decay: longer or shorter, hit by hit
Shapes: off, triangle, ramp up, ramp down, sample & hold; from the top of the bar on PLAY

Sound

A drum part in every pattern, with its own length (polymeters) and its own pattern
Accent: up to 3× as loud · a closed hat chokes the open one
A plate reverb; the delay is the bass's, so the echoes line up
MIDI channel 10, GM notes: BD 36 · SD 38 · LT 45 · HT 50 · CY 49 · OH 46 · CH 42

Lights

Voice keys in their colours; the selected one bright; each flashes as it plays
Muted: blinks slowly · whole kit back: all flash white
Accent blue · roll green · erase red, with the voices it's erasing
CHOMPI amber on this side; it flashes on the beat
